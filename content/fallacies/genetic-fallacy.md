## Definition

The **genetic fallacy** evaluates a claim based on where it came from rather than on the evidence supporting the claim.

## Example

> "That idea came from a conspiracy theorist, so it must be false."

The origin of an idea does not by itself determine whether the idea is true.

## Why It's Fallacious

A claim can come from an unreliable source and still happen to be true. Conversely, a claim from a reputable source can still be false.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Am I evaluating the claim itself, or only where it came from?"
