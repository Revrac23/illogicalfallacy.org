## Definition

A **loaded question** is phrased in a way that assumes something that has not been established, making the respondent appear to accept that assumption by answering the question.

## Example

> "Have you stopped cheating on your exams?"

Both "yes" and "no" appear to accept the assumption that the person has cheated.

## Why It's Fallacious

The question embeds a disputed claim instead of establishing it independently.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What assumptions would I be accepting simply by answering this question?"
