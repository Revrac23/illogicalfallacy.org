## Definition

The **correlation-causation fallacy** occurs when a statistical or observed relationship between two variables is treated as proof that one causes the other.

## Example

> "Ice cream sales increase when drownings increase, so eating ice cream causes drowning."

Both may increase because hot weather increases both swimming and ice cream consumption.

## Why It's Fallacious

Correlation can result from coincidence, a common cause, reverse causation, or other relationships.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What evidence establishes the causal mechanism?"

A relationship between two variables alone does not establish causation.
