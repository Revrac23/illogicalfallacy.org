## Definition

An **appeal to tradition** argues that a belief or practice is correct or preferable simply because it has existed for a long time.

## Example

> "We've always held the meeting this way, so changing the process would be wrong."

The age of a practice does not establish that it is effective.

## Why It's Fallacious

Tradition can provide useful historical information, but it does not by itself establish that something is correct or optimal.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What evidence supports the practice apart from the fact that it is traditional?"
