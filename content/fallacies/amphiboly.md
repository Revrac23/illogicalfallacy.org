## Definition

**Amphiboly** occurs when the grammatical structure of a statement allows multiple interpretations and an argument relies on one interpretation without establishing it.

## Example

> "I saw the man with the telescope."

This could mean that I used a telescope to see the man, or that the man had the telescope.

## Why It's Fallacious

The argument treats an ambiguous grammatical construction as though its intended meaning were unambiguous.

## How to Recognize It

Rewrite the statement in an unambiguous form and ask whether the argument still follows.
