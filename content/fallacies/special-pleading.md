## Definition

**Special pleading** occurs when someone applies a rule or standard to other cases but invents an unsupported exception for their preferred case.

## Example

> "Everyone should provide evidence for their claims, but my claim is based on personal experience, so I don't need evidence."

The standard is being applied selectively.

## Why It's Fallacious

A consistent standard should be applied to comparable cases unless there is a relevant reason for treating them differently.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Would I accept this exception if the same argument were made by someone else?"
