## Definition

The **No True Scotsman** fallacy occurs when a general claim is protected from a counterexample by redefining the relevant category to exclude the counterexample.

## Example

> "No true fan would criticize the team."

> "But Alex is a fan and he criticized the team."

> "Then Alex isn't a true fan."

The definition of "fan" has been changed specifically to protect the original claim.

## Why It's Fallacious

The argument introduces an unsupported requirement for membership only after encountering a counterexample.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Was this definition or requirement established before the counterexample appeared?"
