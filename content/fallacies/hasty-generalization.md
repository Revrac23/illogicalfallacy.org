## Definition

A **hasty generalization** draws a broad conclusion from too few observations or from observations that are not representative of the population being discussed.

## Example

> "I visited two restaurants in that city and both were terrible. The restaurants there must all be terrible."

Two experiences are not enough to establish a conclusion about every restaurant in the city.

## Why It's Fallacious

The evidence may be genuine, but the sample is insufficient to support the scope of the conclusion.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "How large and representative is the sample?"

The broader the conclusion, the stronger and more representative the supporting evidence needs to be.
