## Definition

A **slippery slope** argument claims that allowing or causing one event will inevitably lead to a chain of increasingly serious consequences without adequately establishing that the chain will occur.

## Example

> "If we allow students to use phones during lunch, eventually they'll use them during every class and nobody will pay attention anymore."

The argument assumes a chain of events without establishing that each step will necessarily follow.

## Why It's Fallacious

Consequences can follow from one another, but the argument must establish why the proposed chain is sufficiently likely.

## How to Recognize It

Look for a sequence such as:

> A → B → C → D → extreme outcome

Then ask whether there is evidence supporting each important step in the chain.
