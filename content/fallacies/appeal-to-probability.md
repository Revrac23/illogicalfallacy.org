## Definition

An **appeal to probability** occurs when an argument treats something that is likely or possible as though it must be true.

## Example

> "There's a 90% chance the package will arrive today, so it will definitely arrive today."

A high probability is not certainty.

## Why It's Fallacious

Probability describes uncertainty. A probable outcome can still fail to occur.

## How to Recognize It

Look for a shift from:

> "probably"

or

> "likely"

to:

> "therefore it must be true."
