## Definition

The **Texas Sharpshooter fallacy** occurs when someone searches through data for a pattern, selects a pattern that appears interesting, and then presents it as meaningful without accounting for the other patterns that could have been selected.

## Example

A person records dozens of unrelated measurements, finds a subset that happens to form a striking pattern, and then claims the pattern proves a meaningful connection.

## Why It's Fallacious

Large or complex datasets can contain apparent patterns purely by chance. Selecting the interesting pattern after examining the data can make random coincidence appear significant.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Was this pattern predicted before the data were examined?"

Also ask what other patterns were possible but ignored.
