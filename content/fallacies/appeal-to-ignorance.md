## Definition

An **appeal to ignorance** argues that a claim is true because nobody has proven it false, or false because nobody has proven it true.

## Example

> "Nobody has proven that extraterrestrial life doesn't exist, so extraterrestrial life must exist."

The absence of evidence establishing the claim does not establish the claim itself.

## Why It's Fallacious

A lack of evidence for a proposition is not automatically evidence for its opposite. Whether the absence of evidence matters depends on what evidence we would reasonably expect to find if the claim were true.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Does the argument rely solely on the fact that the opposing claim hasn't been proven?"

If so, the argument may be appealing to ignorance.
