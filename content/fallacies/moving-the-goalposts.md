## Definition

**Moving the goalposts** occurs when someone changes the requirements for accepting a claim after those requirements have been satisfied.

## Example

> "Show me one study proving that the treatment works."

After being shown a study:

> "That's only one study. Show me ten."

After ten studies:

> "Now show me that every major medical organization agrees."

## Why It's Fallacious

The standard for accepting the claim keeps changing rather than being applied consistently.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Was this new requirement part of the original standard of evidence?"

If not, the standard may have been changed after the evidence was presented.
