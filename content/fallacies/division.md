## Definition

The **fallacy of division** occurs when someone assumes that a property of a group or whole must necessarily apply to every individual member or component of that whole.

## Example

> "The company is extremely wealthy, so every employee who works there must be wealthy."

The company's financial resources do not necessarily determine the financial situation of each individual employee.

## Why It's Fallacious

Properties of a whole do not necessarily transfer to each of its parts. A group can have a characteristic that none of its individual members possess, or an individual member can differ substantially from the group as a whole.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Does this property necessarily apply to every individual part of the whole?"

If not, the conclusion may be committing the fallacy of division.
