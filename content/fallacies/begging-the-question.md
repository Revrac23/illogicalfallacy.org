## Definition

**Begging the question** occurs when an argument assumes the truth of its conclusion within its premises instead of independently establishing it.

## Example

> "This policy is the best policy because it is better than all the other policies."

The conclusion that the policy is best is simply restated as a premise.

## Why It's Fallacious

The argument does not provide independent support for its conclusion. It assumes what it is supposed to prove.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Would I have to already accept the conclusion for these premises to work?"

If the answer is yes, the argument may be begging the question.
