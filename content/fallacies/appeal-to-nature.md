## Definition

An **appeal to nature** assumes that something is good, safe, healthy, or desirable because it is natural, or that something is bad because it is artificial or unnatural.

## Example

> "This substance is natural, so it can't be harmful."

Natural substances can be harmful, and artificial substances can be beneficial.

## Why It's Fallacious

Whether something is natural does not by itself determine whether it is good, safe, healthy, or desirable.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What evidence connects being natural with the property being claimed?"

If naturalness is the only support, the argument may be committing an appeal to nature.
