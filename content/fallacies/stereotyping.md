## Definition

**Stereotyping** occurs when a generalized belief about a group is treated as though it necessarily applies to every individual member of that group.

## Example

> "He's a teenager, so he must be irresponsible."

The person's membership in a demographic group does not establish that particular characteristic.

## Why It's Fallacious

Groups can have statistical characteristics without every individual member sharing those characteristics.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What evidence shows that this particular individual has the claimed characteristic?"
