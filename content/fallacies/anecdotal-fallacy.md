## Definition

The **anecdotal fallacy** uses a personal experience or isolated example as though it were sufficient evidence for a broader conclusion.

## Example

> "My grandfather smoked every day and lived to be 95, so smoking can't be that dangerous."

One person's experience does not outweigh broader evidence about health risks.

## Why It's Fallacious

Individual experiences can be informative, but they may not be representative of the larger population.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Is this single example representative of the broader claim?"
