## Definition

A **false cause** fallacy occurs when an argument incorrectly identifies one thing as the cause of another.

## Example

> "The city installed new streetlights, and crime went down. Therefore, the streetlights caused the reduction in crime."

Other factors could have contributed to the change.

## Why It's Fallacious

An observed association or sequence does not necessarily establish a causal relationship.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What alternative causes could explain this outcome?"
