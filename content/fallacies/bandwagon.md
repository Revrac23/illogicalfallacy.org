## Definition

The **bandwagon fallacy** argues that a claim is true, correct, or worthwhile because many people believe it or do it.

## Example

> "Millions of people use this product, so it must be the best."

Popularity does not establish quality.

## Why It's Fallacious

Large numbers of people can be mistaken. The truth or quality of a claim requires relevant evidence rather than popularity alone.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Is popularity being offered as evidence of truth or correctness?"
