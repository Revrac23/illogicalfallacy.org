## Definition

**Poisoning the well** occurs when someone attempts to discredit a person, source, or group before their argument is presented, encouraging others to reject what they say regardless of the evidence.

## Example

> "Don't listen to anything Alex says about the project. He's always been a terrible employee."

Alex's past behavior does not establish that everything Alex says about the project is false.

## Why It's Fallacious

Discrediting a person in advance does not establish that their future claims are false. Each claim should be evaluated based on its evidence and reasoning.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "Is this information being presented to evaluate the argument, or to make me reject the speaker before hearing the argument?"

If the goal is to discredit the source before considering the evidence, poisoning the well may be involved.
