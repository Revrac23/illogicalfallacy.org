## Definition

**Cherry picking** occurs when someone selectively presents evidence favorable to their conclusion while ignoring relevant evidence that points in another direction.

## Example

> "Here are five people who succeeded without attending college. Therefore, college isn't necessary for success."

The argument ignores the many other relevant cases that might contradict the conclusion.

## Why It's Fallacious

Evidence must be evaluated as a whole rather than selectively choosing only the observations that support a preferred conclusion.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What relevant evidence has been left out?"
