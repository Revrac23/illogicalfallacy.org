## Definition

An **appeal to emotion** attempts to persuade by provoking feelings rather than providing relevant evidence for the conclusion.

## Example

> "If you really cared about your family, you would support this proposal."

The statement attempts to connect the proposal to feelings about family rather than demonstrating why the proposal is good.

## Why It's Fallacious

Emotions can be relevant to decision-making, but feeling strongly about something does not by itself establish that a particular conclusion is true or that a particular action is justified.

## How to Recognize It

Ask:

> "What evidence supports the conclusion apart from the emotional reaction?"

If the emotional response is doing the work that evidence or reasoning should do, an appeal to emotion may be present.
