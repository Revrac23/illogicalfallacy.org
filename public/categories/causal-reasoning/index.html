Causal Reasoning

Fallacies in the Causal Reasoning category.

Correlation Does Not Imply Causation

Assuming that because two things are correlated, one must have caused the other.

Domino Effect

Claiming that one event will trigger a chain of events without sufficient evidence that the chain will occur.

False Cause

Incorrectly identifying the cause of an event or outcome.

Post Hoc

Assuming that because one event happened after another, the first event caused the second.
