Weak Induction

Fallacies in the Weak Induction category.

Anecdotal Fallacy

Using a personal experience or isolated anecdote as sufficient evidence for a broader conclusion.

Cherry Picking

Selecting evidence that supports a conclusion while ignoring relevant evidence that contradicts it.

Hasty Generalization

Drawing a broad conclusion from an insufficient or unrepresentative sample.

Stereotyping

Applying a generalized belief about a group to an individual without sufficient evidence.

Texas Sharpshooter

Selecting patterns or evidence after the fact and treating the selected pattern as if it had been predicted in advance.
