Probability

Fallacies in the Probability category.

Appeal to Probability

Treating a probable possibility as though it were certain to be true.

Base Rate Fallacy

Ignoring the general frequency of an event or condition when evaluating specific evidence about it.

Gambler's Fallacy

Believing that an independent random event is more or less likely because previous independent events had different outcomes.

Hot-Hand Fallacy

Believing that a person is more likely to succeed again after a series of successful outcomes, even when the events are independent.
