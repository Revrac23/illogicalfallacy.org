Equivocation

Using the same word or phrase with different meanings within the same argument.

Category: Ambiguity
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

Equivocation occurs when an argument uses a key word or phrase in more than one sense while treating those different meanings as though they were the same.

Example

“A feather is light. What is light cannot be dark. Therefore, a feather cannot be dark.”

The word “light” changes meaning between “not heavy” and “bright.”

Why It’s Fallacious

The argument appears to connect its premises through the repeated word, but the word’s meaning changes along the way.

How to Recognize It

Identify important words that appear multiple times and ask:

“Does this word mean exactly the same thing every time it is used?”
