Domino Effect

Claiming that one event will trigger a chain of events without sufficient evidence that the chain will occur.

Category: Causal Reasoning
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

A domino effect argument claims that one event will inevitably trigger a sequence of additional events, usually ending in a significant consequence.

Example

“If we allow one exception to this rule, everyone will demand an exception and the entire system will collapse.”

The argument does not establish that each step will actually follow from the previous one.

Why It’s Fallacious

A causal chain needs evidence at each important link. The mere possibility of a sequence does not make the sequence inevitable.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“What evidence supports each step in the proposed chain?”
