Tu Quoque

Rejecting criticism by accusing the critic of the same behavior.

Category: Relevance
Also known as: You Too, Appeal to Hypocrisy
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

Tu quoque is a form of ad hominem reasoning that responds to criticism by pointing out that the person making the criticism has behaved inconsistently with their own claim.

Example

“You say I shouldn’t smoke, but you smoke too.”

The critic’s behavior does not determine whether smoking is harmful.

Why It’s Fallacious

A person can make a correct argument even if they fail to follow their own advice. Hypocrisy may be worth discussing, but it does not refute the underlying claim.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“Does the critic’s behavior actually disprove their argument?”

If not, pointing out their inconsistency does not address the substance of the claim.
