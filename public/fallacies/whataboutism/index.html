Whataboutism

Responding to criticism by pointing to another person's wrongdoing instead of addressing the original criticism.

Category: Relevance
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

Whataboutism responds to an accusation or criticism by raising a different accusation against someone else.

Example

“Why did your organization violate the policy?”

“What about the other organization? They violated an even worse policy.”

The second organization’s behavior does not answer the original question.

Why It’s Fallacious

Another person’s wrongdoing may be relevant to a broader discussion, but it does not automatically excuse or refute the original criticism.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“Does the response address the original criticism, or does it introduce a different accusation?”
