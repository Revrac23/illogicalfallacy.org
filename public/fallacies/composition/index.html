Composition

Assuming that what is true of individual parts must also be true of the whole.

Category: Presumption
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

The fallacy of composition occurs when a property of individual parts is assumed to necessarily apply to the whole.

Example

“Every player on this team is an excellent athlete, so the team must be the best team in the league.”

The quality of individual players does not necessarily determine the quality of the team as a whole.

Why It’s Fallacious

Properties of individual components do not always transfer to the system or group they compose.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“Does this property necessarily transfer from each part to the whole?”
