Shifting the Burden of Proof

Demanding that others disprove a claim instead of providing evidence for the claim being made.

Category: Presumption
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

Shifting the burden of proof occurs when someone making a claim attempts to make other people responsible for disproving it.

Example

“I say this device can read minds. Prove that it can’t.”

The person making the claim is responsible for providing evidence that the device can read minds.

Why It’s Fallacious

A claim does not gain credibility merely because others cannot disprove it.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“Who made the claim, and who is being asked to provide the evidence?”
