Red Herring

Introducing an irrelevant issue to distract from the argument being discussed.

Category: Relevance
Type: Informal fallacy

Definition

A red herring is an irrelevant point introduced into an argument to distract from the original issue.

Example

“Why are you concerned about the company’s environmental record? They’re one of the largest employers in the region.”

The company’s employment numbers do not answer the question about its environmental record.

Why It’s Fallacious

The new issue may be interesting or even true, but it does not address the question under discussion.

How to Recognize It

Ask:

“Does this response actually address the original question or claim?”

If the discussion has shifted to an unrelated subject without resolving the original issue, a red herring may be involved.
