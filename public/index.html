Why this site exists

Arguments matter.

Logical fallacies are patterns of reasoning that can make an argument appear stronger than it really is. Learning to recognize them makes it easier to evaluate claims, identify weak reasoning, and have better conversations.

Each entry in this library explains what a fallacy is, shows an example, and describes how to recognize it in practice.
